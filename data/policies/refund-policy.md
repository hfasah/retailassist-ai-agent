# Returns & Refund Policy

*Fictional policy for the RetailAssist demo store.*

## Return window

- You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund.
- Items must be unused, in original condition, and with tags attached. Footwear must be returned in its original box and show no signs of outdoor wear.
- Tents, sleeping bags, and sleeping pads can be returned if they have been set up indoors only and show no dirt or damage.

## Items that can't be returned

- **Final sale items**, including anything marked "Clearance." These are listed as final sale on the product page.
- Items returned more than 30 days after delivery.
- Items that have been used outdoors, washed, or altered (unless they are defective; see below).

## Defective or damaged items

- If an item arrives damaged or develops a manufacturing defect within **1 year of delivery**, we'll repair, replace, or refund it, even if it has been used.
- Report damage on arrival within **7 days of delivery** and include a photo.
- Return shipping for defective or damaged items, or for orders where we sent the wrong item, is **free**.

## How to start a return

1. Contact support with your order number (for example, `ORD-5001`) and the item you want to return.
2. We'll email a prepaid return label and set the order status to **return requested**.
3. Pack the item securely and drop it off with the carrier within **14 days** of receiving the label.

## Return shipping cost

- **Free** for defective, damaged, or incorrect items.
- **$7.95** for all other returns. This is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** once we receive and inspect the return, usually within **3 business days** of it arriving at our warehouse.
- Your bank may take another **5–10 business days** to show the refund.
- The refund covers the item price and the sales tax paid on it. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to our error or a defect.
- Once a refund has been issued, the order status changes to **refunded**.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the size or color you want. If the new item is low on stock, place the new order first so it doesn't sell out.

## Cancellations

- Orders can be cancelled free of charge while they are still **processing** (usually within a few hours of ordering).
- Once an order has shipped, it can't be cancelled, but it can be returned under this policy after it arrives.
- Cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method.

## Price adjustments

If an item you bought drops in price within **14 days of delivery**, contact us and we'll refund the difference once per item. This doesn't apply to clearance or final sale prices.
