# Shipping FAQ

*Fictional policy for the RetailAssist demo store.*

## Where do you ship?

We ship to all 50 US states, including P.O. boxes (standard shipping only). We don't currently ship internationally.

## What are the shipping options and costs?

| Method | Delivery time after shipping | Cost | Carriers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $6.95, or **free on orders of $75 or more** | USPS or UPS |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $14.95 | UPS or FedEx |
| Overnight | 1 business day | $29.95 | FedEx |

The free-shipping threshold is based on the order subtotal before tax and applies to standard shipping only.

## When will my order ship?

Orders placed before **2 p.m. Mountain Time** on a business day usually ship the next business day. Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship on the next business day after that. Delivery times are counted in business days (Monday–Friday) from the day the order ships.

## How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll get an email with the carrier and tracking number. You can also ask our assistant for your order status with your order number (for example, `ORD-5001`). Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after the label is created.

## What do the order statuses mean?

- **Processing**: we've received your order and are getting it ready. It can still be cancelled.
- **Shipped**: the package is with the carrier and on its way.
- **Out for delivery**: the package should arrive today.
- **Delivered**: the carrier has marked the package as delivered.
- **Cancelled**: the order was cancelled before shipping and refunded in full.
- **Return requested**: a return label has been issued and we're waiting for the item to come back.
- **Refunded**: the return has been processed and the refund issued.

## Can I change my shipping address?

Yes, while the order is still **processing**. Once it has shipped, we can't change the address, but you may be able to redirect the package through the carrier's website.

## My tracking says delivered, but I don't have my package.

1. Check around your property, with neighbors, and at your building's mailroom.
2. Wait one business day; carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early.
3. If it still hasn't turned up, contact us within **7 days** of the delivery date and we'll open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or refund.

## My order arrived damaged.

Contact us within **7 days of delivery** with your order number and a photo of the damage. We'll send a free return label and a replacement or refund. See the Returns & Refund Policy for details.

## Why did my order arrive in more than one box?

Larger items like tents and backpacking packs sometimes ship separately. You'll get a tracking number for each package.

## Do you offer in-store pickup?

Not at this time. All orders are shipped.
